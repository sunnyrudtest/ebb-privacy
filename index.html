Ebb privacy policy

Ebb Launcher (com.ebb.launcher) · Last updated 28 October 2026

Ebb is a home-screen app for Android. It has no account and no servers of its own, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking. What Ebb reads stays on your phone, except for the few requests listed under Sent over the internet.

What Ebb reads, and why

Seeing your installed apps is part of being a home screen. Everything else below is off until you allow it in Android, and Ebb works without it. You can turn each one off again in Android's settings or in Ebb ▸ Settings ▸ Permissions.

Sent over the internet

  • Weather: the coordinates of the place shown (your approximate location, or the city you chose) are sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. City names you search for are sent there too.
  • Currency: exchange rates are downloaded from Frankfurter. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Web search and links: when you search the web or open a link from Ebb, it opens in your browser or the app it belongs to, under that service's own privacy policy.

Those services see your IP address, as with any internet request. Ebb sends nothing else, sells nothing and shares nothing with anyone.

Other apps' widgets

If you add another app's widget to Ebb's dashboard, that app draws it and supplies its content, under its own privacy policy. Ebb only places it and can show it in gray; it doesn't read what the widget shows.

Backups

If Android backup is on, your settings, tasks, habits and focus history go to your own Google account backup, like any app's. Chats, scheduled messages, chat tags and chat pictures are left out of backups and phone-to-phone transfers.

Settings ▸ Backup can also save your Ebb setup (look, drawers, tiles, tasks, habits and similar settings) to a file you choose. That file stays wherever you put it; Ebb never uploads it. Chats and scheduled messages are never included.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Ebb keeps lives in its private storage on your phone, protected by Android. You can delete it at any time: Clear all chats in Ripples removes chats (their pictures follow within a day); Android ▸ Apps ▸ Ebb ▸ Storage ▸ Clear storage removes everything; uninstalling Ebb removes everything too. As Ebb has no servers, there is nothing to delete anywhere else.

Children

Ebb is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If Ebb starts using data in a new way, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
